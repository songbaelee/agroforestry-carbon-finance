What we intentionally left out

This first stage still does not include issuance delays, buffers, verification, leakage, farmer payments, financing, discount rates, or investor returns. Those are real and will make the economics tighter. Stage 1 only teaches the basic engine plus one crucial reality: carbon revenue takes time.
